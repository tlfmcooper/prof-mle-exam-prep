## 🏠 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your central hub for exam preparation.

### Quick Stats

Four key metrics are displayed at the top:

1. **Questions Attempted** - Your progress through the question bank
   - Shows: `X/15` questions completed
   - Percentage complete

2. **Overall Accuracy** - Your performance across all questions
   - Shows: Your accuracy percentage
   - Number of correct answers

3. **Study Time** - Total time spent practicing
   - Shows: Hours studied
   - Sessions this week

4. **Study Streak** - Consecutive days of study
   - Shows: Current streak
   - Best streak achieved

### Section Progress

Visual cards showing your mastery level for each exam section:

- **MLOps & Automation** (22% of exam)
- **Model Serving** (20% of exam)
- **Model Development** (18% of exam)
- **Low-code AI Solutions** (18% of exam)
- **Data Collaboration** (12% of exam)
- **Monitoring** (10% of exam)

**Mastery Levels:**
- 🔴 **Novice**: < 3 attempts
- 🟡 **Learning**: ≥ 3 attempts, < 75% accuracy
- 🔵 **Proficient**: ≥ 5 attempts, 75-90% accuracy
- 🟢 **Mastered**: ≥ 10 attempts, ≥ 90% accuracy

## 📖 Practice Mode

Practice Mode allows you to learn at your own pace with instant feedback.

### How It Works

1. **Start Practice**
   - Click "Start Practice" on the dashboard
   - Questions are presented one at a time

2. **Answer Questions**
   - Read the question carefully
   - Select your answer from multiple choices
   - Click "Submit Answer"

3. **Instant Feedback**
   - ✅ Correct answers show in green
   - ❌ Incorrect answers show in red
   - Detailed explanation appears immediately
   - Confidence level is recorded

4. **Navigation**
   - **Next Question**: Proceed to the next question
   - **Previous**: Review the last question
   - **Back to Dashboard**: Save progress and exit

### Tips for Practice Mode

✅ **Read explanations thoroughly** - Even for correct answers
✅ **Take notes** - Keep a separate document for key concepts
✅ **Focus on weak areas** - Revisit topics where you struggle
✅ **Don't rush** - Understanding beats memorization

## ⏱️ Exam Simulation

Exam Simulation replicates the actual Professional ML Engineer exam environment.

### Exam Details

- **Duration**: 2 hours (120 minutes)
- **Questions**: 15 sample questions (actual exam has 50-60)
- **No Feedback**: Answers revealed only after completion
- **Timer**: Countdown visible throughout
- **No Pausing**: Simulates real exam conditions

### How to Use

1. **Preparation**
   - Set aside uninterrupted time
   - Find a quiet space
   - Have notepaper ready

2. **During the Exam**
   - Monitor the timer
   - Flag difficult questions for review
   - Don't spend too long on one question
   - Review all answers before submitting

3. **After Completion**
   - Review your score
   - Study explanations for incorrect answers
   - Check analytics for weak areas
   - Plan focused practice

### Exam Strategies

🎯 **Time Management**: ~2-3 minutes per question
🎯 **First Pass**: Answer easy questions first
🎯 **Second Pass**: Tackle flagged questions
🎯 **Process of Elimination**: Rule out obviously wrong answers
🎯 **Stay Calm**: If stuck, make your best guess and move on

## 📊 Analytics & Insights

The Analytics page provides detailed performance metrics and study recommendations.

### Readiness Score

A comprehensive score (0-100) indicating your exam preparedness:

**Calculation:**
- 50% - Topic Mastery (weighted by exam importance)
- 30% - Overall Accuracy
- 20% - Question Bank Coverage

**Score Interpretation:**
- 🟢 **85-100**: Ready to take the exam
- 🟡 **70-84**: Almost ready, focus on weak areas
- 🟠 **50-69**: Good progress, continue studying
- 🔴 **< 50**: Keep practicing, build fundamentals

### Performance Charts

#### 1. Performance Trends (Last 30 Days)
- Line chart showing daily questions attempted
- Daily accuracy percentage overlay
- Identify patterns in your study habits

#### 2. Accuracy by Exam Section
- Bar chart comparing performance across topics
- Color-coded: Green (≥80%), Yellow (60-80%), Red (<60%)
- Prioritize sections with lowest accuracy

#### 3. Time Distribution
- Pie chart of study time per topic
- Ensure balanced preparation across sections
- Identify over-studied vs. under-studied areas

#### 4. Performance by Difficulty
- Easy, Medium, and Hard question accuracy
- Bars show correct/total attempts
- Track improvement on challenging questions

#### 5. Study Calendar Heatmap
- GitHub-style activity calendar (90 days)
- Darker colors = more activity
- Maintain consistent study streaks

### Weak Areas Alert

Automatically identifies topics below 75% accuracy:
- Lists topics needing attention
- Sorted by exam weight (prioritize important topics)
- Recommended practice questions count
- Quick action buttons

### Predicted Exam Score

Based on weighted accuracy across all topics:
- **Predicted Score**: Your estimated exam percentage
- **Confidence Range**: ±5% interval
- **Pass Likelihood**: Probability of passing (70%+ required)

## 📅 Study Plan Generator

Get a personalized study schedule based on your performance and exam date.

### How to Generate

1. **Navigate to Analytics**
2. **Scroll to Study Plan Generator**
3. **Enter Target Exam Date**
   - Select your scheduled exam date
   - Must be a future date

4. **Set Study Hours per Week**
   - Realistic estimate (e.g., 10-15 hours)
   - Adjustable from 1-40 hours

5. **Click "Generate Personalized Study Plan"**

### Understanding Your Plan

#### Plan Summary
- **Days Until Exam**: Countdown
- **Weeks of Preparation**: Study weeks available
- **Hours per Week**: Your committed hours
- **High Priority Topics**: Count of critical areas

#### Focus Areas by Priority

**🔴 High Priority**
- Accuracy < 60% or very low attempts
- High exam weight topics
- Recommended: 15-20 questions

**🟡 Medium Priority**
- Accuracy 60-75% or moderate attempts
- Medium exam weight
- Recommended: 10-15 questions

**🟢 Low Priority**
- Accuracy > 75% or mastered
- Lower exam weight
- Recommended: 5-10 questions for maintenance

#### Weekly Breakdown

**Phase 1 (First 50% of time)**
- Focus: High-priority topics
- Goal: Build weak area fundamentals
- Daily target: Questions per day

**Phase 2 (Next 30% of time)**
- Focus: Medium-priority + review
- Goal: Reinforce learning
- Mix of new and review questions

**Phase 3 (Final 20% of time)**
- Focus: Final review and practice exams
- Goal: Exam readiness
- Full mock exams and polishing

## 🎯 Tips for Success

### Study Strategies

✅ **Consistency Over Cramming**
- Study 1-2 hours daily rather than 10 hours on weekends
- Build and maintain study streaks

✅ **Active Learning**
- Don't just read explanations - understand WHY
- Explain concepts in your own words
- Teach concepts to others

✅ **Spaced Repetition**
- Revisit topics multiple times
- Review weak areas weekly
- Use the study plan's phased approach

✅ **Balanced Preparation**
- Cover all 6 exam sections
- Don't over-focus on favorite topics
- Weight study time by exam percentage

### Exam-Specific Tips

📌 **Understand GCP Services**
- Vertex AI, AI Platform, AutoML
- BigQuery ML
- Cloud Composer, Dataflow, Dataproc
- Monitoring and logging tools

📌 **Know ML Fundamentals**
- Model selection and evaluation
- Bias and fairness
- Feature engineering
- Hyperparameter tuning

📌 **Grasp MLOps Concepts**
- CI/CD for ML
- Model versioning and monitoring
- Pipeline orchestration
- A/B testing and canary deployments

### Day Before Exam

☑️ **Review study plan summary**
☑️ **Take one final practice exam**
☑️ **Review flagged difficult questions**
☑️ **Get good sleep**
☑️ **Eat well and stay hydrated**
☑️ **Prepare exam environment (if remote)**

### During the Exam

⏰ **Time Management**: Keep to 2-3 minutes per question
🤔 **Read Carefully**: Watch for keywords like "NOT", "EXCEPT", "MOST cost-effective"
✏️ **Flag for Review**: Mark uncertain questions, return later
🎯 **Trust Your Preparation**: Don't second-guess too much

## 🆘 Common Questions

### Q: How many questions should I practice before the exam?
**A:** Aim to complete all 15 sample questions multiple times, achieving 80%+ accuracy consistently.

### Q: What's a good readiness score?
**A:** 85+ means you're ready. 70-84 is close - focus on weak areas for 1-2 more weeks.

### Q: Should I use Practice Mode or Exam Simulation?
**A:** Use Practice Mode for learning (80% of time), Exam Simulation for assessment (20% of time).

### Q: How do I improve my weak areas?
**A:**
1. Focus on understanding explanations
2. Research topics in official GCP documentation
3. Practice related questions multiple times
4. Use the generated study plan

### Q: Can I retake questions?
**A:** Yes! Retaking questions helps reinforce learning and improves accuracy.
